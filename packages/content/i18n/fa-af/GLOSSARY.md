# Dari help translation: terminology

موتر, رخصتی, خانه‌پری. Polite "شما" throughout (the Dutch says "je"). Numbers in Persian digits (۴۰). The key Dutch word
follows in brackets the first time in a lesson; abbreviations (UWV, KVK, cao, IDW) stay in Latin letters.
