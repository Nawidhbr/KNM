# Working on KNM (nederlands.app; repo: knm)

A course for the KNM exam (Kennis van de Nederlandse Maatschappij) of the Dutch civic integration, built on the infrastructure of The Mantiqi (repo Nawidhbr/themantiqi): the same study engine, test, review, word pop-ups, search, PWA, error reporting, CSP and site checks, with KNM content instead of manṭiq.

- Monorepo with npm workspaces: `apps/web` (Astro, static) and `packages/content`. Node 22+. Live at www.nederlands.app (the bare nederlands.app redirects there in the Vercel domain settings) (`SITE_URL` in `apps/web/src/config/site.ts`; never hard-code the domain). The site name is `SITE.name` ("KNM"); change it there only.
- Run `npm run check` before committing: the tests of the study rules (`apps/web/test/progress.test.js`, vitest), the content validation, the build, `scripts/check-site.mjs` (broken links, missing title/description/canonical/og:image, other than one h1, bad JSON-LD, two Dutch pages with one title) and the Playwright tests in `e2e/` (locks and stars, review, word pop-ups, main pages on desktop and phone without sideways scrolling or script errors). GitHub Actions runs the same on every push.
- Dutch (nl) is the source language: the exam is in Dutch. Every text field is `{ nl, en? }`; a missing translation shows the Dutch (`tx`/`txl` in `packages/content/src/index.ts`). Interface text lives in `apps/web/src/i18n/<code>.json` (nl is the base, others merged over it); never write interface text into a page. Live languages: `LIVE_LOCALES` (nl, en); ar, Dari (fa-af), tr and ti are listed as "in preparation" in `SITE.languages`. Every URL starts with the locale (`/nl/...`); `/` redirects to `/nl/` (vercel.json).
- Content (`packages/content`), validated by `scripts/validate.ts` against `src/schema.ts`, which writes `generated/*.json`; the site imports only the generated bundles:
  - `themes/index.json`: the eight themes of the eindtermen, in order. A theme with a course has `themes/<id>.json`: lessons numbered 1, 2, … (one crucial action of the eindtermen each: `handeling`), each with a situation, a few short paragraphs, the rule to remember, sources and a test of 3–8 questions (three options, `answer` the index of the right one, `eindterm` the code it tests, ids `<theme>-<n>-<k>`). Themes without a course show as "in preparation".
  - `topics/<slug>.json`: the knowledge base. Sections, the eindtermen, the theme (none for the page about the exam), sources and `checked` (the date the facts were checked).
  - `glossary/<locale>.json`: words for the pop-ups (forms as they occur, a plain explanation, optional topic). `lib/gloss.ts` marks them once per text block.
  - `news.json`: "What is changing", each item with its official source.
  - `reviews.json`: reviews a qualified teacher has actually given (`<theme>-<n>` or `topic:<slug>`, by, date). Everything else shows "Concept · nog niet nagekeken door een docent" (`components/ReviewBadge.astro`); keep that marking visible.
- The eindtermen are those of Staatscourant 2024, 15802 (in force since 1 July 2025). Never use real exam questions (DUO keeps them secret) and never copy DUO's practice exams; never call the site official. Facts that change (the prime minister's name, amounts) are not written into lessons; topics name where to check them.
- Questions and translations may be drafted with AI, but every question is checked against its eindterm and an official source before it goes in.
- Study path (rules in `apps/web/src/lib/progress.js`, the same file as The Mantiqi, sent inside `/<locale>/study-data.js` and run by `scripts/head-study.js` before paint; change a rule only there and only with a test): every theme is open from the start; inside a theme the lessons open one by one, each when the one before has at least two stars. Stars from a score out of 10: 1 from 4, 2 from 7, 3 from 9 (with five questions: four right passes). Scores stay in the browser (`scores` in localStorage). Lesson addresses: `/<locale>/study/<theme>/<n>/`, score key `<theme>-<n>`.
- Review (`/practice/`, tikrār on The Mantiqi; `scripts/practice.js` unchanged): only passed lessons can be chosen; its pool is `/<locale>/practice/questions.json`.
- House style "Stedelijk": Mondriaan through Swiss modernism (`SITE.palette` "mondriaan", `styles/palettes.css`, over the layout of The Mantiqi in `styles/global.css`, copied, with KNM additions in `styles/knm.css`). Crisp white, a strict grid of thin black rules (light in dark mode), square corners, square tiles for steps, headings in Inter Tight 800, and small exact blocks in the red, white and blue of the flag with orange. Cards are a hairline box under a 2px black rule. Orange (`--red-fill`, #FF7A00) marks the current item and the main action and always carries black text (white on orange is 2.6:1); flag red (`--red`, #AE1C28) is the accent and the kickers; flag blue (`--blue`, #21468B) is the steps field on the home page and the passed tiles, with white text. The home panel is one composition: a dominant white field with the title and a small red block, beside a blue field with the steps under an orange and a white block. Under the header a thin band of blocks between black rules. Buttons are not set in capitals (readability for inburgeraars). The logo is a small Mondriaan composition with a K in the orange block (`--stijl-*` colours, which never change; `public/favicon.svg`, the PNGs in `public/icons/`, the share image `public/og/default.jpg`). Colours are CSS tokens; never hard-code a colour in a page. Reading text in the knowledge base is Literata; the study portal uses Atkinson Hyperlegible.
- Scripts: no inline scripts (strict CSP in `vercel.json`). A page script is a file in `apps/web/src/scripts/`, imported with `?url` and loaded with `<script is:inline src={url}>`; data goes in `data-vars`. A new outside resource needs a CSP entry.
- SEO (`components/Seo.astro`): one `<h1>` per page; titles within 65 characters, descriptions about 155; every page has an og:image (`public/og/default.jpg`). Titles are written for what people type ("KNM oefenen", "KNM-examen", "oefenvragen", "oefentoets", "inburgering"): the main pages have their own search title and description (`seo` in the UI dictionaries, by path, read with `seoFor`), themes and lessons use `study.themeSeoTitle`/`study.lessonSeoTitle`, and other titles end in `meta.titleSuffix` ("KNM oefenen"). Structured data (`lib/structured-data.ts`): Organization (with logo) and WebSite on the home page, Course on theme pages, Article on topics, BreadcrumbList; ids use the canonical URL. Indexing is controlled by `INDEXING=on` in Vercel (previews never index); robots.txt names the sitemap only then. After a production deploy, GitHub Actions (`indexnow.yml`, `scripts/indexnow.mjs`) sends the sitemap to IndexNow (Bing and others) once a day, and nothing while the site is noindex; the key file is `public/<key>.txt`.
- PWA: `pages/sw.js.ts` precaches the pages of `lib/routes.ts`; add new page types there.
- Selling access (Mollie): one payment (`config/offer.js`: €7.99 incl. 21% VAT, 365 days) opens every theme after the free ones (`freeThemes`: theme 1; the knowledge base is always free). No accounts and no database: the code is the Mollie payment id with an HMAC signature (`api/_access.js`, key `ACCESS_SECRET`); `api/checkout.js` creates the payment and then sets its redirectUrl to `/<locale>/unlock/?code=…`; `api/redeem.js` asks Mollie whether that payment is paid and not refunded and answers the end date; `api/mollie-webhook.js` mails the buyer the link and a confirmation of the purchase (Resend), once (`mailed` in the payment's metadata). The buy page `/<locale>/unlock/` (`scripts/unlock.js`) keeps `access` ({ code, until }) in localStorage; the head script sets `data-access` on every page (hides the offers, `[data-buy]`) and `data-paywall` on the pages of a theme in `paid` (from study-data.js) without access, which shows `.paywall-note` instead of the lesson. The rule is `hasAccess` in `lib/progress.js` (tested in `test/access.test.js`, with the codes); `e2e/unlock.spec.ts` answers /api/redeem itself. A simple lock by choice: the lessons are in the page. Selling is on only with `PAYMENTS=on` and the business details in `config/company.js` (`lib/offer.ts`, `selling()`); the functions also need `MOLLIE_API_KEY` and `ACCESS_SECRET` (and `RESEND_API_KEY` for the mail). Then the footer links the terms (`/<locale>/terms/`, `terms` in the dictionaries) and the privacy page adds `privacy.payments`. Both pages are noindex and out of the sitemap. Keep the terms, the mail and the privacy text in step with the offer.
- Contact: `api/contact.js` mails through Resend (env `RESEND_API_KEY`, optional `CONTACT_FROM`/`CONTACT_TO`); without a key the reader's mail program opens. Errors in readers' browsers go to `api/error.js` (Vercel function log, `client-error`).
- Not yet ported from The Mantiqi: accounts (Supabase), previews of locked lessons, the editing screen (Keystatic), the news finder and uptime workflows. Port them from themantiqi when needed.
